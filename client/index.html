SAMAJIK SAMIKSA

SAMAJIK SAMIKSA is an independent online journal that publishes articles, research papers and thoughtful discussions on society, governance, public affairs, culture and related topics.

All published content is freely available to every visitor without creating an account or signing in.

Why Google Sign-In is Used

Google Sign-In is completely optional. It is used exclusively for newsletter subscriptions. Visitors do not need to sign in to browse or read any publication.

Users Can

  • Read all articles without signing in.
  • Read all research papers without signing in.
  • Browse publications freely.
  • Optionally subscribe to the newsletter using Google Sign-In.
  • Manage newsletter subscription preferences.
  • Unsubscribe at any time.

Information Requested

If a user chooses to sign in using Google, only the minimum information required for authentication and newsletter subscription is used:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Google Account ID

This information is not used for advertising, public profiles or social networking.

No Login Required

The homepage and all published content are publicly accessible. Signing in is required only for users who voluntarily subscribe to receive newsletter updates.
